Business Sales Performance Analytics Dashboard
📌 Project Overview
This project was completed as part of the Data Science & Analytics Internship (Task 1) with Future Interns.

The objective of this analysis is to transform raw, unorganized e-commerce transaction data into an interactive, client-ready sales performance dashboard. This dashboard helps business owners quickly identify top-selling product categories, analyze market share by region, and discover strategic areas for business growth.

📊 Dashboard Preview
Sales Performance Dashboard Figure 1: Interactive Sales Performance Dashboard built in Microsoft Excel.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
Microsoft Excel / Google Sheets
Data Cleaning Techniques: Text to Columns delimiter separation, formatting data types (Dates and Currency), removing blank values.
Data Analysis: Pivot Tables for dynamic data aggregation.
Data Visualization: Pivot Charts (Clustered Column Charts for category comparison, Pie Charts for regional market share).
Interactivity: Dynamic Slicers with cross-report connections for real-time data filtering.
💡 Key Business Insights
After cleaning and analyzing the dataset, three major operational insights were uncovered:

Technology is the Powerhouse: The Technology category drives the highest overall sales revenue (over $836,000), significantly outperforming Office Supplies and Furniture.
West Region Dominates: Looking at the geographic market share, the West Region is the business's strongest territory, making up the largest slice of total sales.
Office Supplies Lagging: While Office Supplies represents a high volume of individual transactions, it generates the lowest total revenue share for the company.
🚀 Strategic Recommendations
Based on the insights above, the business should consider the following actions:

Double Down on the West: Allocate more marketing budget to the West region to capitalize on existing high customer demand.
Optimize Office Supplies Pricing: Evaluate the pricing strategy or bundle options for Office Supplies to increase the average order value (AOV) for that category.
Investigate Furniture Margins: Although Furniture brings in solid revenue, further analysis should be done on shipping costs and discounts to ensure it is actually profitable.
